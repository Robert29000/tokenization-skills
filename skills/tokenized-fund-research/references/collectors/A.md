# Your task — collector A: Issuer docs & data

Collect what the issuer and data providers publish about the product.

1. **Docs site**: try `llms.txt` and the `.md` page versions first. Read the product pages, the FAQ, and the subscription / redemption pages.
2. **Product page and factsheet**: AUM, NAV, yield, fees, minimums, subscription and redemption mechanics, settlement times, cut-offs.
3. **Portfolio composition**, with an as-of date: asset classes and allocation %, top holdings, maturity ladder, duration, cash buffer.
4. **Issuer API**, if there is one (e.g. Superstate's NAV and holdings endpoints): primary data for NAV and holdings.
5. **rwa.xyz / DeFiLlama**: AUM, holder count, chains, contract addresses, holder concentration. Cite these only for on-chain metrics; their service-provider fields can be stale.
6. **Third-party research**: Rating reports (S&P, Moody's, Particula…), attestation / proof-of-reserve reports, VaultStreet reports. Cite them as secondary sources.
7. **Wayback Machine** snapshots, to date changes in manager, providers, fees or terms.

Your facts feed report sections 1, 2 (underlying assets), 4.2 (venues, concentration) and 6.3 (redemption, NAV, reporting). Service-provider names you find here are leads for B to confirm against the legal documents. Note them, but don't treat them as final.
