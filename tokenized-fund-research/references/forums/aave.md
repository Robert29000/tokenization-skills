# Aave

What to look for:
- `[ARFC] Onboard <TICKER> … to Aave Horizon` threads. Since 2026 Horizon onboarding requires a business case, a **LlamaRisk risk assessment posted as a reply inside the ARFC thread**, an Aave Labs technical assessment and a Snapshot vote.
- **Horizon launch assets** (USTB, USCC, USYC, JAAA, JTRSY, VBILL) went live without individual ARFCs or LlamaRisk asset reviews. For these, read the Horizon launch and "Horizon Weekly Highlights" threads, and say in the report that no asset-specific review exists.
- "LlamaRisk – Monthly Community Update" and Chaos Labs parameter posts.
- An ambiguous ticker: add `Horizon` as a second term with `--all-terms`.

Who posts here: LlamaRisk (asset reviews inside ARFC threads, monthly updates), Chaos Labs (market-risk parameters: caps, LTV), Aave Labs and the Aave Chan Initiative (technical assessments, process updates), Gauntlet (historically).
